# WATonomous Quest Book - Winter 2026 (W26)

## Term Objectives Summary

The objectives for Winter 2026 focus on securing funding, improved marketing to multiple faculties and clubs, and flushing out the website and meking it more engaging. The following key areas are critical for WATonomous' continued success:

1. **Funding**
   - Secure funding from WEEF, MEF, Deans, Engsoc, and external sources.
2. **Marketing**
   - Create social media content to showcase progress.
   - Market WATonomous to multiple faculties and clubs to increase awareness around campus.
3. **Website**
   - Include more photos and videos to make the website more engaging.
   - Maintain and improve the website whenever possible.
